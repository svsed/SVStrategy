# Competitor Analysis

# The competitive narrative

The market is converging. Almost every major technology-services narrative now includes AI transformation, agents, reinvention, outcomes or AI-native engineering.

| Competitor | Narrative | Strength | SV opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thoughtworks | Rebuild / Rewire / Reimagine | Engineering + transformation | Be more explicit about business leverage |
| EPAM | AI/Run.Transform | Enterprise engineering scale | Smaller, faster, diagnostic entry |
| Globant | AI Pods | Human + AI service model | Own intelligent business systems |
| Cognizant | Agentic Enterprise Services | Huge enterprise process depth | Bespoke workflow-first proposition |
| Searce | AI Outcome Engineering | Strong problem-first narrative | Differentiate through orchestration model |
| Publicis Sapient | Real AI returns | Business transformation | More technical/systemic, less agency |
| Quantiphi | AI-first digital engineering | AI product depth | Stronger business-system story |
| BCG / BCG X | Value from AI | Strategy + build | Productized, execution-native alternative |
| Accenture | Reinvention | Scale + breadth | Personal, focused, experimental |
| Genpact | AI-led operations | Process + managed services | Cross-system orchestration, not outsourcing |

## The benchmark to beat

Searce is especially relevant because “bring us a problem, see the outcomes” is close to the customer-led behavior SV needs. SV should avoid copying the wording and instead create a distinctive interaction around **finding business leverage**.

## Competitive whitespace

Not “AI consulting”.
Not “agent development”.
Not “digital transformation”.
Not “IT services”.

The whitespace is the **intelligent business system**: the layer that makes an existing business ecosystem able to sense, reason, act and learn across boundaries.
