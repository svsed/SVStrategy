# Collated Insights

# The strategy that survives the evidence

1. **AI adoption is not the problem. Business redesign is.**
2. **The workflow is the unit of transformation.**
3. **System boundaries are where value gets stuck.**
4. **Human-in-the-loop is a design variable, not a failure state.**
5. **Existing ERP/CRM/SCM should usually be orchestrated, not replaced.**
6. **Proof must show business movement, not AI activity.**
7. **The first engagement must be small enough to buy and large enough to prove value.**
8. **SV needs reusable IP, not a custom-services snowflake every time.**
9. **Regional governance and resilience are part of system design.**
10. **The website should help a CEO discover a problem they can now see clearly.**

## Strategic choice

Sell the outcome. Demonstrate the system. Productize the pattern. Expand through adjacent workflows.
