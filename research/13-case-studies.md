# Case-study portfolio

Build three anchor stories first.

## 01 Multi-country manufacturer
CRM + SCM + ERP orchestration.

Story: fragmented demand/order/supply signals create coordination drag. SV builds an intelligent workflow across the existing estate.

Proof to collect: order cycle time, exception volume, planner hours, OTIF, margin leakage.

## 02 Growth-constrained B2B business
Demand → conversion → account expansion.

Proof: lead response, qualification, win rate, sales cycle, expansion revenue.

## 03 Scale-constrained operations business
Manual expert workflow → supervised agentic workflow.

Proof: revenue/FTE, cost/transaction, cycle time, exception rate.

## Story format

**Signal → friction → hidden cost → new system → human role → outcome → expansion**

Never publish a hypothetical result as a real case. Label concept studies clearly until client evidence exists.
