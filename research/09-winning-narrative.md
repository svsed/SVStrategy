# Shaping SV's Winning Narrative

# The story

### Opening
**Your business already has the systems. The problem is that the work still moves between them manually.**

### Tension
ERP knows orders. CRM knows customers. SCM knows supply. Finance knows cash. People know the exceptions. AI knows patterns. But the business still has to coordinate them.

### Shift
SV connects these systems into intelligent workflows that can sense, reason and act.

### Human promise
Machines move routine work. People keep judgement, accountability and exception handling.

### Proof
Show a concrete before/after workflow with one business KPI.

### CTA
**Bring us one workflow worth redesigning.**

## Narrative ladder

1. Business pain
2. Hidden system friction
3. Value opportunity
4. Intelligent workflow
5. Human checkpoint
6. Pilot
7. Outcome
8. Expansion

The website should never lead with a list of AI technologies.
