# Real Customer Needs

# The demand underneath the AI demand

Customers rarely wake up wanting “agents”. They wake up with a business constraint.

| Customer need | Observable symptom | SV lever | KPI family |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grow | Leads/accounts leak | Demand + conversion intelligence | pipeline, win rate, revenue |
| Convert | Slow response / weak context | Next-best-action orchestration | sales cycle, win rate |
| Scale | Headcount follows revenue | Agentic workflow | revenue/FTE, cost/transaction |
| Operate | Exceptions consume experts | Exception orchestration | cycle time, touchless rate |
| Decide | Data exists but decisions wait | Decision systems | decision latency, forecast accuracy |
| Reshape | AI pilots stay isolated | AI production fabric | time-to-production, reuse |
| Start | Legacy complexity would be created | AI-native backbone | time-to-market, cost-to-serve |

## Evidence

PwC's 2026 global CEO survey found 56% of CEOs reported no significant financial benefit from AI to date, while only 12% reported both revenue and cost benefits. Deloitte found 48% had introduced AI without redesigning workflows or roles. These two findings define the gap SV should attack: **AI adoption without business redesign**.

## Latent need

The latent need is usually one layer below the stated request.

“Automate CRM” → “reduce revenue leakage and response time.”

“AI for supply chain” → “reduce exception latency and improve resilience.”

“Deploy agents” → “move work without multiplying management overhead.”

“Modernize ERP” → “make the business flow across systems instead of inside system boundaries.”

SV's discovery process should always translate the technology request back into a measurable business outcome.
