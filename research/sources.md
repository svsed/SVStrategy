## Data limitations

Direct Crunchbase MCP extraction was attempted but the connected account is not entitled to the Crunchbase MCP service. The research therefore uses public Crunchbase reporting where cited, rather than private database extracts. No ZoomInfo connector is available in the current tool environment, so ZoomInfo-specific contact/account enrichment is not represented as verified.

The account directory is therefore an evidence-backed qualification system and target-account hypothesis set, not an exhaustive commercial database.

## Interpretation

All quantitative claims retain the date, geography and population of the cited source. Strategy conclusions are synthesis, not source claims.
