# Geo-Political Scenarios

# Regional Customer Realities

Geography changes the **constraints around the same transformation**.

## US

The commercial question is increasingly tied to growth, productivity and speed. Buyers can have large enterprise estates and expect fast pilots, security and clear ROI.

**SV angle:** outcome-first, bounded pilot, existing-stack integration, measurable value.

## EMEA

AI adoption is rising unevenly. EU enterprise AI use reached 20% in 2025, with 55% of large enterprises using AI versus 19% of SMEs. Regulation and data governance are part of the buying context. The AI Act became applicable in August 2026, with further high-risk provisions phased later.

**SV angle:** trusted orchestration, human oversight, governance, explainability, regional data architecture.

## APAC

APAC is not one market. India is a high-momentum technology and AI services market; Japan has deep industrial automation; Singapore is a regional technology/logistics hub; China has distinct cross-border data requirements.

**SV angle:** operational leverage, manufacturing/supply-chain orchestration, local data architecture and regional scale.

## Geopolitical opportunity

The more fragmented the supply chain, data environment and regulatory landscape becomes, the more valuable **orchestration across boundaries** becomes.

SV should therefore treat resilience as a system property: alternate suppliers, regional data paths, human escalation, policy-aware agents and observable decision trails.
