SV Strategy 2.0

Business Landscape

SV should qualify customers by economic shape: Complex Operator, Growth-Constrained, Scale-Constrained, Transformation-Ready Enterprise and New Business.

Strategic view

Primary buyers include CEO/Owner, COO, CRO and CIO/CTO. Qualification centers on value, workflow complexity, fragmentation, readiness, urgency and pilotability.

The detailed evidence and working notes are maintained in research/02-business-landscape.md.

SV design rule

Automate what machines should do. Augment what humans should own. Orchestrate everything in between.
