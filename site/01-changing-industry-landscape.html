Changing Industry Landscape

The IT/ITeS business model is being rewritten

The 10-year shift

2015-2018 - Labor and project scale. Application development, infrastructure, maintenance, outsourcing and cost arbitrage dominated the commercial model.

2019-2021 - Cloud and digital modernization. Cloud migration, SaaS, digital products, data platforms and managed services became central.

2022-2024 - GenAI experimentation. Copilots, RAG, content generation, software-development acceleration and functional pilots moved AI into enterprise budgets.

2025-2026 - Agentic pivot. The unit of value is moving from a task or project to an intelligent workflow. McKinsey reports more than 80% of surveyed C-suite executives were already piloting agentic AI in 2025, while Deloitte's 2026 research shows 48% of respondents had introduced AI without redesigning workflows or roles.

2027-2031 - Adaptive business systems. The likely direction is productized services, reusable orchestration, outcome-linked economics, human-supervised agents and continuous evolution. This is a strategic horizon, not a claim that every enterprise will mature at the same speed.

Strategic implication

SV should not compete for generic application delivery. It should occupy the space between business problem and intelligent workflow.

The new unit of value

Old: people × hours × projects.

Emerging: systems × intelligence × outcomes.

The opportunity is to build repeatable IP around the second model.
