SV Customer Intelligence Map

The account-level intelligence system

Every target account should have one living record.

Identity

Company, region, industry, revenue, employees, ownership, business model, geography.

Pressure

Growth, margin, conversion, scale, resilience, compliance, service, new market.

Business system

Value chain, workflows, systems, data, integrations, manual work, exceptions.

Intelligence

AI maturity, existing pilots, agents, data readiness, governance, human decision points.

Opportunity

High-value workflows, expected value, complexity, pilotability, expansion path.

Buying system

Executive sponsor, economic buyer, technical buyer, users, blockers, incumbent partners, procurement process.

Competitive system

Likely alternatives, current vendors, internal build capability, status quo cost.

Engagement

Entry offer, proof required, pilot KPI, next action, confidence level, expansion hypothesis.

Account health

Use a simple state model:

Unknown → Researched → Hypothesis → Engaged → Diagnosed → Pilot → Proven → Expanded

The map is the backbone of SV's prospecting, sales, marketing and case-study pipeline.
