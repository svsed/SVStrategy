Digital Touchpoints - 90 Days

Three-month digital strategy

Month 1 - Make the category visible

Website: proposition + leverage map.

LinkedIn: evidence-led POV 2-3x/week.

Email: CEO/COO/CIO problem-specific sequences.

Instagram: system maps and short orchestration stories.

Facebook: selective repurposing.

Month 2 - Show the machinery

Website: five orchestration stories.

LinkedIn: demos, before/after workflows, system diagrams.

Email: profile-specific hypotheses.

Instagram: signal→think→act→human checkpoint.

Month 3 - Convert

Website: account-specific pages + opportunity assessment.

Email: named-account campaigns.

LinkedIn: proof and executive stories.

Commercial CTA: Bring us one workflow worth redesigning.

Content system

One research insight → one executive post → one system diagram → one short video → one email → one website proof story.

This creates a compounding content engine instead of five separate social-media strategies.
