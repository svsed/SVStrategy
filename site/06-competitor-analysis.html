The competitive narrative

The market is converging. Almost every major technology-services narrative now includes AI transformation, agents, reinvention, outcomes or AI-native engineering.

The benchmark to beat

Searce is especially relevant because “bring us a problem, see the outcomes” is close to the customer-led behavior SV needs. SV should avoid copying the wording and instead create a distinctive interaction around finding business leverage.

Competitive whitespace

Not “AI consulting”.

Not “agent development”.

Not “digital transformation”.

Not “IT services”.

The whitespace is the intelligent business system: the layer that makes an existing business ecosystem able to sense, reason, act and learn across boundaries.
