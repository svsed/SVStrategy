Underlying ThumbRule

See → Think → Act → Amplify → Learn

See

Sense signals from systems, people, documents, customers, markets and external events.

Think

Reason with context, goals, policies, constraints and history.

Act

Execute, recommend, communicate, update or route.

Amplify

Scale successful patterns across functions, geographies and business units.

Learn

Measure outcomes, capture exceptions, update policies and improve continuously.

Why it matters

This is more than a process diagram. It is the design logic behind every SV system.

If a proposed AI solution cannot explain what it sees, how it reasons, what it can do, where a human decides and how the system learns, it is not yet a complete business system.
