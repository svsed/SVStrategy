Evidence

PwC's 2026 global CEO survey found 56% of CEOs reported no significant financial benefit from AI to date, while only 12% reported both revenue and cost benefits. Deloitte found 48% had introduced AI without redesigning workflows or roles. These two findings define the gap SV should attack: AI adoption without business redesign.

Latent need

The latent need is usually one layer below the stated request.

“Automate CRM” → “reduce revenue leakage and response time.”

“AI for supply chain” → “reduce exception latency and improve resilience.”

“Deploy agents” → “move work without multiplying management overhead.”

“Modernize ERP” → “make the business flow across systems instead of inside system boundaries.”

SV's discovery process should always translate the technology request back into a measurable business outcome.
