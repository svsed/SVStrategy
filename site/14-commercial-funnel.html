Commercial Funnel & Integrations

Commercial system

Funnel

Discover → search, LinkedIn, referrals, thought leadership, interactive diagnostic

Qualify → problem, value, complexity, readiness

Diagnose → executive session + system map + opportunity scan

Prove → 4-8 week pilot tied to one KPI

Transform → scale the winning pattern

Amplify → expand across functions/geographies

Learn → continuous optimization and opportunity discovery

Recommended stack

HubSpot CRM + website analytics + forms + calendar + email automation + lead scoring + proposal/workflow tracking.

Add account-based tooling only after qualification is stable.

Required CRM fields

Profile type, revenue band, employee band, geography, systems estate, primary friction, workflow, estimated value, executive sponsor, urgency, AI maturity, pilotability, competitor, next action.

Commercial principle

Do not sell “an AI project”. Sell a measurable workflow transformation with a clear path to expansion.
