Specific Interactions, Stories & Flow

The experience

Interaction 1 - Find Your Leverage

1. Choose business intent.

2. Choose friction.

3. Select current systems.

4. Estimate transaction/workflow volume.

5. Select human judgement level.

6. Generate leverage map.

Interaction 2 - System Story

Animate one workflow from fragmented state to orchestrated state.

Before: CRM → email → spreadsheet → ERP → human chase.

After: signal → context → agent reasoning → approved action → system updates → human exception.

Interaction 3 - Executive proof

Show only four things:

  • baseline
  • intervention
  • human role
  • outcome

Interaction 4 - Pilot builder

Visitor chooses one workflow and gets:

  • systems involved
  • data needed
  • candidate agents
  • HITL checkpoints
  • KPI
  • 4-8 week pilot shape

This becomes the bridge from website to commercial conversation.
